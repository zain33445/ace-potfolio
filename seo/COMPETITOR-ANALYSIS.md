## Backlink profile shape (post-disavow)

- Pre-disavow: 750+ referring domains, ratio dominated by low-quality directories and comment spam.
- Post-disavow (2026-08-19 upload, 293 domains): 457 referring domains remain, but DR is still 14 — meaning most retained links are low-value.
- **Re-pull in ~4-6 weeks** (target: week of 2026-10-01) to see whether Google has actually stopped counting the disavowed set.

Competitor link profiles (estimators.us in particular) are dominated by construction blogs, cost-calculator embeds on third-party sites, and trade-publication mentions. That's the profile shape to earn — not more directories.

## Threat: AI-generated content flood

Multiple new competitors in 2025-2026 are launching AI-generated content sites in this niche. They rank briefly then drop. The differentiator that survives:
- Real named authors with credentials
- Original data (calculator outputs, cost-per-region tables built from real project data)
- Downloadable proof (sample takeoffs, filled RFP templates)

ACE should double down on the "real US team" positioning in bylines, author bios, and About page — the exact signals Google's 2024-2026 helpful content updates reward.
