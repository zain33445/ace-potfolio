# Part 2 — Their DR 59 is bought, and it's already decaying

This is the most important finding in the audit.

**Their anchor text profile:**

| Anchor | Ref. domains | Links |
|---|--:|--:|
| "Construction Estimating Services" | 996 | 5,282 |
| "construction takeoff services" | 579 | 4,594 |
| "Electrical Estimating Services" | 327 | **13,753** |
| "Commercial estimating services" | 240 | 430 |
| "lumber takeoff services" | 219 | 306 |
| "mechanical estimating services" | 160 | **12,749** |

996 separate domains linking with the identical exact-match commercial phrase is not a distribution that occurs naturally. Neither is 13,753 links on one anchor.

**And then there is anchor #8 — 218 referring domains carrying this as their link text:**

> "Back when I first launched worldestimating.com, I had no idea how crucial SEO would be. A friend introduced me to SEOExpress.org… my domain authority jumped from DA 10 to DA 45 in just two months! Sales increased by an incredible 190%…"

That is a link-vendor testimonial, syndicated across 218 domains as anchor text pointing at their site. It documents the purchase in their own words.

**Their top referring domains by DR** are free-host and CDN junk — blogspot.com (100 links), cloudfront.net (153), wixsite, weebly, squarespace, heylink.me, pages.dev — not editorial placements. The handful of real ones (Wikipedia ×2, LA Times ×1, Business Insider ×2, BBB ×1, PRNewswire ×14 paid releases) are a rounding error against 18,326 links.

**Decay:** 5,423 all-time referring domains → **1,838 live**. Two thirds are already gone.

### What this means for you

1. **Do not try to match their link volume.** You cannot buy your way there safely, and you have already been through this — you disavowed 293 domains on 2026-08-19.
2. **Their ranking position carries real risk.** A site whose top-3 rankings rest on 996 exact-match paid anchors is exposed to any link-spam update or manual action. If that lands, the SERPs they hold vacate.
3. **Position yourself to be the one standing there when it does.** Clean profile, real pages, real data. That is the whole strategy.

# The plan

## Phase 1 — Weeks 1–4: take the cheap keywords (biggest, fastest return)

Build 12 trade estimating pages at ~1,000–1,200 words each, modelled on your existing `/cost-estimating/` template (which already carries FAQPage schema and reads well):

`electrical` · `hvac` · `plumbing` · `mechanical` · `drywall` · `roofing` · `masonry` · `concrete` · `millwork` · `sitework` · `flooring` · `steel/metals`

Plus four standalone commercial pages: `/commercial-estimating-services/`, `/residential-estimating-services/`, `/material-takeoff-services/`, `/outsourcing-construction-estimating/`.

**Do not copy their state matrix.** 121 near-duplicate state pages is exactly the scaled-content pattern flagged in `seo/content-eeat-audit-2026-09-03.md`, and it is the fragile half of their site. Take the trade dimension only.

## Phase 2 — Weeks 2–3: own the uncontested lane

Optimise the existing shop drawings, 3D rendering and permit set pages against their real keywords, and add `/rebar-detailing-services/` as a dedicated page. Zero competition from the category leader.

## Phase 3 — Month 2: publish pricing, or an honest substitute

You lose the price-comparison searcher by default today. You do not have to publish a flat rate — but a "what estimates cost and what drives the number" page, with the calculator embedded, captures that intent without anchoring you to $200.

## Phase 4 — Ongoing: build the data moat

More pages like the ADU cost study — per-type, per-region cost benchmarks with real division-level numbers. That is what earns links you did not buy, and citations in AI answers. It is also the only durable answer to a DR 59 that was purchased.

## Not recommended

Buying links to close the DR gap. It is what their profile is made of, it is 66% decayed already, and you spent August cleaning up exactly this.

## One caveat on the numbers

Ahrefs KD and traffic estimates are third-party models, not Google data — treat them as directional. The keyword *list* is reliable (those are observed rankings); the difficulty scores are an estimate of how hard the SERP is. Search Console remains the first-party source for your own performance.
